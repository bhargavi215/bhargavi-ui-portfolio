03 / INCLUSIVE DESIGN

Accessibility is built in

⌨

Keyboard-first interaction

Visible focus indicators, semantic controls, a skip link, and arrow-key navigable code tabs.

◉

Clear feedback

Explicit form labels, programmatically associated errors, and polite live announcements.

▦

Responsive design

Fluid layouts, readable typography, and horizontally scrollable tables on narrow screens.

Designed with WCAG 2.1 AA principles in mind. Formal conformance requires manual and automated testing, including axe and assistive technologies such as JAWS or NVDA.
